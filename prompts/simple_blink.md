# Test 01 — Simple blink

## Goal
A 5-second / 50-frame anime loop with minimal motion so temporal consistency is easy to judge.

## Character
Adult anime woman, blonde hair, blue eyes, relaxed friendly expression, shoulders-up portrait, simple neutral background.

## Motion
- Frames 01–10: neutral, tiny natural breathing/hair movement
- Frames 11–14: eyelids gradually close
- Frames 15–17: eyes closed
- Frames 18–21: eyelids gradually open
- Frames 22–38: neutral, subtle breathing/hair movement
- Frames 39–42: second quicker blink
- Frames 43–50: return smoothly to the initial pose

## Consistency rules
Camera, face proportions, hairstyle, clothing, lighting and background remain fixed. Only eyelids, a tiny amount of hair, and subtle breathing should change. Frame 50 should visually approach frame 01 for a clean loop.
